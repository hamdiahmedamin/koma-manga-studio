# Koma Manga Studio

## Register

product

## Users

People building manga books from their own pictures, including the owner using Android and Chrome. The editor must be usable as an installed phone app and as a desktop workspace. It stays free and works offline after installation.

## Product Purpose

Arrange pictures into pages, edit panels and lettering, manage several books, preview reading order, and export finished books. Preserve existing books and pictures through updates.

## Brand Personality

Creative, precise, capable. Keep Koma's established ink, paper and restrained lime identity. The user explicitly selected a denser professional workspace for this overhaul.

## Anti-references

Avoid forcing the user into a sparse beginner-only workspace. Avoid long undifferentiated settings lists that hide panel tools. Do not turn the editor into a marketing page or add decorative animation that competes with the artwork.

## Design Principles

- Give composition tools a clear, permanent home.
- Offer presets and direct manipulation together. The user confirmed shape presets plus editable corner points.
- Keep pictures, panel geometry and lettering independently editable.
- Make work reversible, preserve content and support reusable layouts.
- Adapt the professional workspace to phone touch controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable text, named controls, visible keyboard focus and large touch targets on mobile. Existing keyboard navigation, reduced-motion preferences and system zoom must remain usable. No additional specialist accessibility requirements have been specified.

## Language support

The user requested Arabic text support. Lettering needs automatic and explicit right-to-left direction, Arabic font fallbacks, intact diacritics, and consistent output in the editor, reader and exports.
