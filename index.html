A story starts with a frame.

Your pictures stay in this browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

On a phone, use the bottom toolbar for Pictures, Designer and Text. Choose Panel to drag panels, or View to scroll and zoom without changing your layout. Tap the three dots beside the page strip for page actions.

  1. Bring your pictures. Upload JPG, PNG or WebP images. Pick a panel, then click a picture to place it. Desktop users can drag and drop.
  2. Find your rhythm. Choose Freeform to drag and resize panels, or start with a template. Add up to 16 panels, overlap them, and adjust layer order. Use the position fields for precise placement.
  3. Make it yours. Set panel borders, rounded corners, page margins and paper color. Rotate or flip pictures and adjust brightness, contrast, saturation and opacity.
  4. Add your words. Add speech, thoughts or a caption. Select a bubble to edit its font, weight, alignment and colors. For speech, choose the tail’s side, position, length and slant in Text. Drag it to position it.
  5. Keep your cast consistent. Open Pictures, then Characters. Create a character, link up to six reference pictures, and keep appearance and story notes beside your pages. References are saved with your project, separate from printed pages.
  6. Build your book. Add, duplicate and reorder pages using the strip below the canvas. Open Pages (inside the three-dot menu on phones) for a full organizer with multi-select, move, duplicate, remove and Undo.
  7. Keep and share it. Save an editable project file. Use Read for a clean book preview with swipe navigation and reading direction. Download the whole book as CBZ for comic readers, export any page as PNG, or choose Download PDF to save the whole book directly. Print book opens your device’s print dialog; use A5 paper with no margins.

Pictures are resized to a maximum of 2000 pixels on their longest side. B&W and Ink are image filters; this editor does not redraw photos or generate AI artwork. Browser autosave is convenient, but a downloaded project is your backup.

Your studio. One tap away.

Add Koma to your home screen to launch it like an app. Once offline setup finishes, you can create and edit without a connection.

  1. Open this website in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap Share (or the browser menu, then Share).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, keep Open as Web App enabled if shown, and tap Add.
  4. Launch Koma using its new home-screen icon.
  1. Open this website in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap Add to home screen in Designer → Book, or open Chrome's ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.
  4. Launch Koma from your home screen or app drawer.
